How it works

  1. Pick the period

    From the panel you choose a period from 1 to 366 days, or rely on the automatic reports.

  2. The AI writes the report

    Charts and commentary on your store, written from the data of the period you chose.

  3. Read, download, act

    Open the report in the panel, download the PDF and data, and start from the action plan.

What it does

  1. When customers buy

    The days and hours with the most orders, so you can schedule campaigns and promotions at the right moment.

  2. The buying journey

    From the visit to the recommendations seen and clicked, up to the order: where customers drop off and where recommendations help.

  3. Products and categories to push

    Which products get few views, which convert well and which categories bring orders.

And more

  1. Customer segments

    Your customers split into segments, with the recommended action for each one.

  2. Cart and pairings

    How many items per order and which products are bought together.

  3. 30-day action plan

    Concrete actions, each one checked against your store's data.

Frequently asked questions

What is Insights?

An AI-written report on your store, with charts, commentary and a 30-day action plan. You'll find it next to the dashboard (clicks, orders and revenue from recommendations; conversations, outcomes and chat satisfaction; queues and operator times) and “Ask your store”: questions about orders, customers, products and revenue, with answers as text, table or chart and reports you can rerun in one click.

How often do reports arrive?

The weekly and monthly reports run automatically and are included in your plan; you schedule them from the panel and an email lets you know when they're ready.

Can I ask for a report on a different period?

Yes: from 1 to 366 days, for 200 credits. Reopening a report you've already generated costs nothing.

How can I trust the advice?

Every action in the plan is checked against your store's data and says what it was checked on. If the data isn't enough, the report isn't generated and you don't pay.

Can I download the report?

Yes, as a PDF; the chart data can also be downloaded as CSV.
