How it works

  1. Upload a photo

    The product arrives on Shopify as a draft, with price and quantity if you give them: you check it and publish it.

Frequently asked questions

How do I create AI product images for Shopify?

You upload the product photo from the Neurocart panel, pick sector, background and framing, and generate the images. You can then retouch them with text or a brush.

Can I generate product descriptions with AI?

Yes. The AI writes the title, short description, long description, product type and tags in your store's language; for each field you choose whether the AI writes it or you fill it in.

Are products published automatically?

No. They arrive on Shopify as drafts: you check them and publish them.

How much does it cost?

It's pay as you go with credits, which every plan includes each month and which don't expire. If a generation fails, the credits go back to your balance. The price list is on the Pricing page.

Do I need to know design software?

No. You pick background and framing from the panel, with no editing software and no changes to your theme code.
