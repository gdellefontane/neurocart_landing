What if I run out of conversations?

  1. They renew every monthYour plan's conversations start over at each renewal.
  2. Add packs of 10You pay for them with credits; they don't expire and add to the ones in your plan.
  3. Automatic top-upIf you turn it on, when only a few are left Neurocart buys a pack on its own, with credits or your saved card: chat never stops.
  4. At zero? No surprisesIf they ran out completely, new chats wouldn't start: the customer sees a notice inviting them to contact your store. Chats already open carry on.

Can I cancel whenever I want?

Yes, you cancel anytime.

How do I pay?

The subscription and top-ups are paid by card, through Stripe, from the Neurocart panel. Automatic top-up uses the card you save.

Neurocart is invite-only: you leave your email and we write to you.

The Enterprise plan is tailored: volumes, SLA and dedicated integrations. Write to sales@neurocart.it.
