How it works

  1. Install the app

    You install the Neurocart app on your Shopify store and connect the store to the panel.

  2. Add the block in the theme editor

    Add the Recommended products block to your product page and choose the type: similar products, bought together, complementary products (“complete the look”) or recommended for you.

  3. Read the results

    The dashboard shows clicks, orders and revenue attributed to recommendations.

What it does

  1. Similar products

    Features, tags and description close to the product being viewed.

  2. Bought together

    What customers who bought this product choose.

  3. Complete the look

    A different kind of product that completes the one being viewed (complementary products).

And more

  1. Recommended for you

    A personal selection for each signed-in customer, based on their purchase history.

  2. Updated every night

    Suggestions are recalculated every night, and recent orders count more.

  3. Cards in your theme's style

    Cards inherit your theme's product card, recognized automatically, or you use a Neurocart style.

Frequently asked questions

How do I show related products on Shopify?

Install the app, then add the Recommended products block on the product page from the theme editor and pick the type. No code needed.

What is the difference between related and recommended products?

Related products are similar to the one being viewed; recommended products are a personal selection for a signed-in customer, from their purchase history. Neurocart offers both, plus bought together and complementary products.

How do I add “you may also like” or “complete the look”?

Add the Recommended products block, choose Similar products (or Cross-sell from cart in the Cart drawer recommendations block) and give the block the title “You may also like”. For “complete the look”, choose Complementary products.

Do I need a developer?

No. You install the app, connect the store to the panel with a code and choose your settings. Your theme code stays untouched.

Related products not showing on Shopify?

A related products block can stay empty when it is not added to the product template of your theme, or when a store or a product is new and there is little history to learn from. With Neurocart you add the block from the theme editor and pick the type; similar products start from your catalog (features, tags, description), so they can appear even with few orders, and the other types are refreshed every night from real purchases.
