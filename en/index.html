Frequently asked questions

Does it work with my theme?

Yes. You add the blocks from the Shopify theme editor. Cards can inherit the style of your theme's product card, recognized automatically, or use one of the six Neurocart styles.

Do I need a developer?

No. You install the app, connect the store to the panel with a code and choose your settings. Your theme code stays untouched.

Where does my data go?

Each store has its own model, isolated from the others. We handle Shopify's GDPR compliance webhooks: deleting a customer or a store propagates across the whole platform. We are an Italian company. Read the privacy notice.

Can I manage more than one store?

Yes. One account, several Shopify stores, with owner and collaborator roles. Each store has its own plan and credits, and your team can follow several stores from the same console.

What happens if the bot can't answer?

If someone on your team is online, the customer can ask to talk to them; otherwise they leave a message and you get an email. If nobody replies within the time you set, the chat goes back to the bot.

How much does a conversation cost?

Each plan includes a quota of conversations: 30 on Base, 100 on Pro. Beyond that, you buy packs of 10 from the panel, which don't expire. We count per conversation, not per message.
